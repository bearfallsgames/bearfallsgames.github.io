About

Bear Falls Games is a one-person studio. Every game here is designed, built, tested, and supported by the same developer, which means the decisions that shape how our games feel are made by someone who games daily.

Minesweeper Madness

The classic puzzle game with modern controls and features. Convenient inputs that cut down on clicks, alternate rulesets, hexagonal boards, optional edge wrapping, and boards that run infinitely in every direction, with high score tracking across every ruleset, so a five-minute break and a long sitting both count for something.

The focus is on familiar games done properly: controls that get out of the way, options that respect how differently people like to play, and support that doesn't stop at the release date.
